## Rules: No Data-Path Computation Outside the Graph

The actual data-path computation (matmuls, activations, reductions that produce the output) must happen inside STeP graph operators, not in PyTorch.

**Allowed outside the graph** (for setup / routing metadata):
- Tensor initialization: `torch.randn`, `torch.zeros`, `torch.ones`, `torch.arange`, `torch.manual_seed`
- Shape arithmetic: `//`, `%`, `ceil`, integer math on dims
- Routing metadata: `@` / `torch.matmul` for router logits, `torch.topk` for expert selection, `torch.softmax` ONLY for computing small expert-weight scalars that get loaded as constants — NOT for data-path softmax
- Constructing selection tensors: building multihot/onehot tensors for `SelectGen`

**Forbidden** — these must be STeP ops:
- Activation functions: `F.silu`, `F.relu`, `F.gelu`, `torch.sigmoid` (use `UnaryMap` with `map_fn.Silu()` etc.)
- Data-path softmax: `F.softmax`, `torch.softmax` on data tensors — decompose into Exp → Broadcast → Accum(Add) → RowWiseSum → RepeatStatic → Div in STeP
- Math on data tensors: `torch.sqrt`, `torch.rsqrt`, `torch.log` (use `UnaryMap`)
- Data routing: `torch.gather`, `torch.scatter`, `torch.where` (use `FlatPartition` / `FlatReassemble`)

If the output of your graph was computed in PyTorch and just wrapped in a Store, that is cheating and will be rejected.
