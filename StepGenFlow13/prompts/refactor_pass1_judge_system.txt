{requirement_2_section}## Requirement {out_shapes_req_number}: Stream-shape invariant on ``out_shapes``

Every entry in every ``out_shapes`` tuple — {out_shapes_call_sites_phrase} — must be a STeP tile-stream shape with **rank >= 3**: at least 1 stream dim followed by exactly 2 tile dims (``tile_row``, ``tile_col``).

- ``out_perms[i]`` (when not ``None``) must have the same length as ``out_shapes[i]`` — i.e., a permutation over ``range(len(out_shapes[i]))``.

Flag any ``out_shapes`` literal in the function body whose entries you can statically determine to have rank < 3, or any ``out_perms`` whose tuple length does not match the corresponding ``out_shapes`` entry's rank.
